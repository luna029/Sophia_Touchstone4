Our Story

North Star Bakery started in 2018 as a small family kitchen in our hometown. We wanted to bring warm, fresh, and handmade bread to our local neighbors, commuters, and families. Today, we are proud to be a favorite gathering spot in the neighborhood.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe good food starts with honest ingredients:

  • Local Grain: We buy flour directly from regional organic farmers.
  • Natural Fermentation: We do not use artificial additives or fast yeast.
  • Pure Butter and Milk: We only use fresh dairy from local cows.
